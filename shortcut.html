Share-sheet shortcut

Once this is set up: in any app, tap Share on a post, choose bruh, and the media saves straight to Photos. It works for single posts, boards and profiles.

Your personal address (it includes your passcode, so don't share it):

Open the main page and enter your passcode first.

Build it (about 2 minutes)

  1. Open the Shortcuts app, tap +, and name it bruh.
  2. Tap the ⓘ (details) button, turn on Show in Share Sheet, and set it to receive URLs and Text.
  3. Add the action URL Encode (it encodes the Shortcut Input, the link you shared).
  4. Add the action Text. Paste your address from above, then at the very end insert the variable URL Encoded Text.
  5. Add Get Contents of URL (it uses the Text from the step above).
  6. Add Split Text, set to split by New Lines.
  7. Add Repeat with Each (it repeats over the split text).
  8. Inside the repeat, add Get Contents of URL using Repeat Item.
  9. Still inside the repeat, add Save to Photo Album (Recents).
  10. Done. Try it: open a post in its app, tap Share, then bruh. The first time, iPhone will ask for permission to reach the site. Tap Always Allow.

If it saves nothing, open the shortcut, tap ▶ on a copied link, and look at what the first Get Contents of URL returns. An error message there (like "Wrong passcode") tells you what's wrong.
